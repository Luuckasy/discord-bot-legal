Privacy Policy

CLCodeTest · Last updated 2026-11-20

This policy explains what data the CLCodeTest Discord application ("the bot") processes, why, and how long it is kept.

1. Data we process

  • Roblox game data: Roblox user ID, username and display name, join/leave times and session length, server identifiers, and the text, category and technical details of bug reports sent from inside our games.
  • Discord data: in channels the bot is configured to read (bug-report channels), the content of messages and attachments posted there, the author's Discord user ID and username, and message, channel and server IDs.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, e-mail addresses or messages from channels the bot is not configured to read.

2. How we use it

Only to receive, organize, de-duplicate and post bug reports and player activity to the development team's Discord channels, so we can find and fix problems in our games. We do not sell data, show ads, or use it to build profiles.

3. Storage and retention

The service runs on Railway. Grouping of duplicate reports is kept in memory for up to 24 hours and is erased whenever the service restarts. Reports that are posted to Discord remain in those Discord channels under Discord's own Privacy Policy until they are deleted by the server staff.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with Discord (to post the reports) and with our hosting provider (Railway) to run the service. We do not share it with anyone else, except when required by law.

5. Your choices

You can avoid having your Discord messages processed by not posting in the bug-report channels. Server admins can remove the bot at any time. To request deletion of a report about you, contact the developer through the Discord server where the bot is installed, and we will delete the related messages.

6. Children

Our games and this bot follow the age requirements of Roblox and Discord. We do not knowingly collect more data from children than described above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer through the Discord server where the bot is installed.
