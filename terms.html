1. What the bot does

Index-Tan helps the development team of our Roblox games handle player feedback. In the bug-report and suggestion channels that the server staff set up, it reacts to acknowledge each report, helps the team classify, group and track reports, marks them when they are fixed and released, and lets the developers reply to a report to ask for more details. In the servers of our games that the developer has set up, it also reads the public channels to write an anonymous hourly summary of how the community feels about the game, collects the answers the staff gives to players' questions into a knowledge base about each game, and helps the staff with moderation: it reads the server rules and the moderation channels the developer lists, summarizes how the staff applies the rules, and points out messages that may break the rules or people who may need help, for the staff to review. The bot never punishes, deletes or contacts anyone. It also relays bug reports and gameplay telemetry sent from inside our Roblox games to the development team's Discord channels.
